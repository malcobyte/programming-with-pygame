# Final Reflection

Write a paragraph (a few sentences each) for every prompt. Be
specific — reference actual moments, actual bugs, actual weeks, not
general statements like "it was fun."

1. **What are you most proud of** building across this whole course?
2. **What was the hardest concept** to learn, and how did you actually
   get through it? (Not "I studied harder" — what specifically
   helped: a talking point, an exercise, a particular bug that finally
   made something click?)
3. **What would you tell yourself** back in Week 1, if you could?
4. **What do you want to learn next?** This could be more pygame,
   something about a specific mechanic your capstone didn't have time
   for, or something else entirely.
